# Regulatory & Standards Equivalents — AU, UAE, Singapore

Purpose: when tailoring a CV toward a specific country, surface genuine transferable regulatory/compliance experience in terms the local reviewer recognizes — without ever claiming direct local experience that doesn't exist.

**Hard rule, repeated from SKILL.md**: this is for *framing existing real experience as transferable*, never for claiming direct experience with a body/law/standard the candidate hasn't actually worked with. "Analogous to" / "directly transferable to" language is fine. Stating the local body's name as if it were on the candidate's actual experience list is not.

**Caveat**: regulatory landscapes change. Treat this table as a starting map to check and refine, not a final authority — confirm current names/scope before relying on it for a live application.

---

## Financial services / prudential & conduct regulation

| Australia | UAE | Singapore |
|---|---|---|
| APRA (prudential regulation) | CBUAE (Central Bank of the UAE) | MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore) |
| ASIC (conduct, markets, corporate) | DFSA (DIFC) / FSRA (ADGM) | MAS (dual mandate — conduct + prudential) |
| RBA (central bank, monetary policy, payments) | CBUAE | MAS |

## AI / technology governance

| Australia | UAE | Singapore |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary AI Ethics Principles (national) | CBUAE Responsible AI Guidance (2026); DFSA/FSRA Enabling Technologies guidelines | MAS FEAT Principles; Model AI Governance Framework (IMDA/PDPC) |
| Privacy Act (APP principles) | UAE Data Protection Law | PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act) |

## Cross-jurisdictional / international standards (state as-is, no local substitution needed)

- ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems)
- ISO/IEC 27001 (information security)
- NIST AI RMF (US-origin but internationally referenced)

These are recognized across all three markets under the same name — do not attempt to "localize" them; just ensure they're prominent if the JD references AI governance/risk maturity.

---

## How to apply this in a CV

1. Identify regulatory/compliance experience in the master profile (e.g. "engaged with APRA prudential standards," "ASIC conduct reporting").
2. Check the JD for the target country's equivalent body/standard.
3. If genuinely transferable, add a framing phrase — e.g. *"Prudential risk governance experience directly analogous to CBUAE's regulatory framework"* — rather than just leaving the Australian-specific term unexplained for a UAE reviewer, or worse, restating it as CBUAE experience outright.
4. If the JD requires demonstrated experience with a specific local law/regulator the candidate has never engaged with (even indirectly), that's a real gap — report it in SKILL.md Step 5's gap list, don't bridge it with equivalence framing.
